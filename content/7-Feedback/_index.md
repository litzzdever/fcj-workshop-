### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The FCAJ program environment is friendly and open. Mentors and fellow participants were ready to help when I got stuck, so I felt comfortable asking questions and discussing ideas.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentors explained things clearly and encouraged me to research and experiment on my own before asking. The admin team provided good support with information, materials, and events, which made the learning process smooth.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The program fits my Artificial Intelligence major well, especially the parts on AI/ML, the RAG chatbot, and AWS AI services. It also introduced me to new areas such as serverless, containers, analytics, and DevOps.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I learned to use many AWS services through hands-on practice, read official documentation, troubleshoot errors, and control costs. Beyond technical skills, I also improved teamwork, blog writing, and report writing.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
People in the team and the program supported each other and shared experience and materials. The team project helped me understand how to split work and coordinate when building a complete system.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program gave me the chance to attend workshops and knowledge-sharing events with AWS experts and companies, which was a big plus for me.

### Additional Questions

- **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  Practicing directly on AWS and working on a team project with a real product, rather than only studying theory.
- **What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**  
  Adding more guidance or lab accounts so interns can practice without worrying about unexpected costs.
- **Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why or why not?**  
  Yes, because it builds a solid cloud foundation, has a clear roadmap, and offers a positive learning environment.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- More project review or small-group mentoring sessions to get feedback earlier.
- I would like to keep taking part in AWS Study Group and FCAJ community activities in the future.
- Thank you to the FCAJ team and mentors for supporting me throughout the program.
